Performance Management

Enterprise performance planning, monitoring, reporting, and improvement.

Domain Code: performance-management

Domain ID: 48D519D6-3320-4C28-BF54-6D74136C26CF

8 linked controls

10. Mandatory Signed Role Approval

Preventive Control Primary Active

performance-management-mandatory-signed-role-approval

Description: Requires all employees to have a signed Role & Responsibility (R&R) document on file before performance metrics are set.

Objective: Ensure performance expectations are formally documented and agreed upon prior to evaluation periods.
Evidence: Signed R&R documents with timestamped approval records in the HRIS repository.

20. Annual Weighting Calibration Review

Directive Control Primary Active

performance-management-annual-weighting-calibration-review

Description: Requires an annual review of weighting logic to ensure alignment with current organizational strategy.

Objective: To maintain relevance and accuracy of performance weights as business priorities shift.
Evidence: Annual calibration meeting agenda and attendance record showing leadership review of weight allocations.

30. Development Plan Weighting Methodology Definition

Directive Control Primary Active

performance-management-development-plan-weighting-methodology-definition

Description: Establishes formal rules requiring specific development plan tiers to correspond with defined performance weighting ranges.

Objective: Ensures consistent application of weightings across roles based on strategic development goals rather than subjective adjustment.
Evidence: Approved policy document outlining the mapping logic between development plan levels and role weighting percentages.

40. Role Weighting Deviation Justification Record

Directive Control Primary Active

performance-management-role-weighting-deviation-justification-record

Description: Mandates documented rationale when weighting allocation deviates from standard matrices due to development plan changes.

Objective: Prevents arbitrary weighting adjustments by requiring traceable links between development needs and performance expectations.
Evidence: Audit logs containing specific justifications linked to approved development plan modifications for the role.

50. Manager Alignment Certification on Development Weightings

Directive Control Primary Active

performance-management-manager-alignment-certification-on-development-weightings

Description: Requires managers to certify annually that assigned role weightings align with approved individual development plans.

Objective: Promotes accountability and transparency in how career development strategies influence performance evaluation criteria.
Evidence: Signed certification records from management confirming adherence between weighting structures and development plan scopes.

60. Mandatory Three-Year Employee Development Plan

Directive Control Primary Active

performance-management-mandatory-three-year-employee-development-plan

Description: Requires creation of a formal three-year development roadmap for all eligible employees.

Objective: Ensures long-term career planning is initiated and documented across the workforce.
Evidence: Signed 3-year development plan documents accessible in HR management system.

70. Strategic Alignment Verification on Development Milestones

Directive Control Primary Active

performance-management-strategic-alignment-verification-on-development-milestones

Description: Requires development milestones to be explicitly mapped against organizational strategic objectives.

Objective: Aligns individual growth trajectories with corporate strategic direction.
Evidence: Documented linkage matrix or system fields mapping development goals to strategic pillars.

80. Annual Development Plan Refresh Cycle

Directive Control Primary Active

performance-management-annual-development-plan-refresh-cycle

Description: Mandates annual review and updating of the three-year development plan to reflect current career status.

Objective: Maintains relevance of development goals within the strategic horizon over time.
Evidence: System logs or signed forms demonstrating annual update approvals for employee plans.

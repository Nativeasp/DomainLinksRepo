## 1.0 Context

### Objective

**1.1** This policy establishes a standardized framework for managing employee performance and professional development to ensure that individual contributions are aligned with organizational strategic objectives.

**1.2** It provides practical guidance on the requirement for formal role agreements, the calibration of performance weightings, and the maintenance of long-term development roadmaps.

### Principles

**1.3 Formal Agreement**: Performance expectations are only valid when formally documented and agreed upon by both the employee and their manager prior to any evaluation period.

**1.4 Strategic Alignment**: All performance metrics, weighting logic, and development milestones must be directly traceable to current organizational strategic mandates.

**1.5 Consistency and Objectivity**: Weighting methodologies shall be standardized across roles to prevent subjective adjustments and ensure equitable evaluation based on strategic goals.

**1.6 Long-term Growth**: Professional development is treated as a multi-year journey, requiring documented roadmaps that are refreshed annually to maintain relevance.

**1.7 Managerial Accountability**: Managers are held accountable for certifying the alignment between an employee's growth trajectory and their performance evaluation criteria.

### Accountability and Transparency

**1.8** All core performance records, including signed Role & Responsibility (R&R) documents and development plans, shall be stored in the HRIS repository and accessible to the employee and authorized management.

**1.9** Documentation access is critical to ensure that employees understand the basis of their evaluations and the expectations for their professional growth.

**1.10** Governance through annual certifications and calibration reviews supports executive oversight and ensures fairness across the organization.

### Strategy

**1.12** Planning responsibilities are driven by the Executive Strategic Mandates, ensuring that individual performance targets support corporate pillars.

**1.13** A defined review and approval path is required for all weighting changes to ensure leadership alignment before metrics are finalized.

**1.14** This policy promotes transparency by requiring published mandates and documented justifications for any deviations from standard weighting matrices.

**1.15** The framework supports foundational organizational goals by linking individual milestones to strategic objectives via a formal linkage matrix.

## 2.0 Application

**2.1** This policy applies to all eligible employees and managers within the organization involved in the performance evaluation and professional development process.

## 3.0 Policy

### Role Approval and Expectations
**3.1** All employees shall have a signed R&R document on file before any performance metrics are set for an evaluation period.
**3.2** R&R documents must be timestamped and stored within the HRIS repository to serve as the baseline for performance measurement.
**3.3** No performance evaluation shall be conducted or finalized without a verified, current R&R agreement in place.
**3.4** R&R shall provide the majority of weight of performance evaluations with each role also be weighted

### Weighting Calibration and Methodology
**3.4** An annual review of all performance weighting logic shall be conducted to ensure continued alignment with the organizational strategy.
**3.5** The calibration process must result in formal documentation, including meeting agendas and attendance records, demonstrating leadership review of weight allocations.
**3.6** A formal methodology for mapping development plan tiers to performance weighting ranges shall be maintained and applied consistently across all roles.

### Weighting Deviations and Justification
**3.7** Any deviation from standard weighting matrices due to specific development plan changes requires a documented rationale.
**3.8** All justifications for deviations must be traceable and explicitly linked to approved modifications in the employee's development plan.
**3.9** Audit logs of these justifications shall be maintained and made available for compliance and fairness reviews.

### Managerial Certification
**3.10** Managers are required to certify annually that assigned role weightings align with the employee's individual development plan.
**3.11** This certification must be provided as a signed record confirming adherence between weighting structures and the scope of the development plan.
**3.12** Failure to provide this certification shall trigger a mandatory review of the performance evaluation criteria for the affected role.

### Long-term Development Planning
**3.13** All eligible employees shall maintain a formal three-year development roadmap documenting their career trajectory and growth goals.
**3.14** Development milestones within the roadmap must be explicitly mapped against organizational strategic objectives via a linkage matrix.
**3.15** The three-year development plan shall be refreshed annually to reflect current career status, achieved milestones, and shifting strategic horizons.

### Quality Assurance
**3.28** The HR Governance team is responsible for tracking all required documents (R&Rs, Certifications, Plans) to ensure quality and compliance.
**3.31** Executive sponsors must be kept informed of the results of annual calibration reviews and overall alignment trends.

## 4.0 Consequences

**4.1** Non-compliance with this policy may result in the invalidation of performance evaluations or the delay of merit-based adjustments.

**4.2** Managers who fail to provide required certifications or justifications may be required to undergo mandatory governance training.

**4.3** Persistent failure to adhere to documentation and alignment requirements may lead to disciplinary action in accordance with organizational HR policies.

## 5.0 Foundational Records and Documents
- Signed Role & Responsibility (R&R) documents
- Annual Weighting Calibration records (Agendas/Attendance)
- Development Plan Weighting Methodology document
- Role Weighting Deviation Justification logs
- Manager Alignment Certification records
- Three-Year Employee Development Plans
- Strategic Alignment Linkage Matrix
